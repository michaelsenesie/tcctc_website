About Our Centre

Empowering Our Local Community Through Technology

Torma Community Computer Training Centre (TCCTC) was established to bridge the digital divide in Torma, Bum Chiefdom. We offer structured computer literacy training along with high-quality printing of documents, photocopying, larminating, binding, printing of lacoste and shirts, caps, blazers, badges, certificates, flyers, invitation cards, mobile money transactions services and secretarial services.

Whether you are a student aiming for academic excellence, a professional building new career skills, or a local resident needing business support, TCCTC provides hands-on guidance, modern computer equipment, and dependable services.
